Weekend Trip to Amalfi

I am planning a two-day trip to Amalfi in southern Italy. It is a small town on a steep coast, famous for its clear blue sea, colorful houses, and sweet lemons.

What to Pack

I will travel light, so I only need a few important things.

  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • A swimsuit and a towel
  • Sunglasses and sunscreen
  • A camera
  • A power plug adapter for Italy

Activities

  1. Visit the Cathedral of Amalfi in the town center.
  2. Walk along the seaside and enjoy the view.
  3. Eat fresh seafood pasta for lunch.
  4. Take a boat trip along the coast.
  5. Try a glass of limoncello in the evening.
