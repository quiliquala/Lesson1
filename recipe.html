Amatriciana Pasta

This is my favorite pasta dish. It has a rich tomato sauce with pancetta, onion, and a little chili, and it is easy to make at home.

Ingredients

This recipe makes two to three servings.

  • 160 g spaghetti or bucatini
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1/2 onion, thinly sliced
  • 40 g pancetta, diced
  • 3 medium tomatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 20 g grated pecorino cheese

Instructions

The sauce takes about 20 minutes, so boil the pasta while it simmers. For another version of this dish, see this recipe website.

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan and cook the onion until soft.
  2. Add the pancetta and one dried chili, and cook until the fat turns clear.
  3. Pour in about 50 ml of white wine, then simmer for a few minutes.
  4. Add the tomatoes and simmer for about 15 minutes until thick.
  5. Boil the pasta in salted water, drain it, and toss it with the sauce.
  6. Stir in half of the cheese, add salt and pepper, and top with the rest.
