Manila, the Philippines

Manila is the capital of the Philippines. It is a busy city that mixes old Spanish history, warm people, and delicious local food.

Three places worth visiting

  • Intramuros
  • Fort Santiago
  • Rizal Park

A One-Day Trip Around Manila

Published on October 10, 2026

I started the day in Intramuros, the old walled city. I walked along the stone streets and visited Fort Santiago, which was full of history and quiet corners.

In the afternoon I relaxed at Rizal Park and ate halo-halo, a cold and sweet dessert that was perfect for the hot weather. One day was short, but it showed me the charm of the city.

Read more on the official Philippines travel guide.

Travel Tips

  • Wear light clothes, because the weather is hot and humid.
  • Use a taxi app to move around the city easily.
  • Start early to avoid the heat and the heavy traffic.

Local Tip

In Intramuros, you can ride a kalesa, a traditional horse-drawn carriage. Agree on the price before you start the ride.
